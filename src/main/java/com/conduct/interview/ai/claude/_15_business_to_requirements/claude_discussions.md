# Discussion topics — _15_business_to_requirements

- How specific does a functional requirement need to be before it's
  "checkable"? Is there a test for that, or is it just judgment?
- Latency, scale, residency, identity — is that list complete, or are
  there other infra constraints (cost ceiling? uptime SLA?) that belong
  in the same up-front conversation?
- Who actually owns finding out the residency/regulation answer — the
  developer, or someone on the business side who talks to legal?
- At what point does "lightweight notes are enough" stop being true? Is
  it about audience (internal vs customer-facing) or about data
  sensitivity, or both?
